# DialogueFoundry model license audit

Audited 2026-11-06 against the implementation's pinned metadata. These are card
claims, not a grant from DialogueFoundry or a conclusion that dataset terms always
apply identically to trained weights or generated audio. Resolve that distinction
with the relevant rights holder where unclear. No weights are included in Git.

unrestricted or infer that separate download cures this issue. Cards marked
"See URL" or "See LICENSE file" are incomplete permission evidence. Some models
were fine-tuned from Ryan; follow those training references as well as the final
model card. Noncommercial dataset labels are material review flags, not something
the application's GPL grant can override. Attribution/share-alike obligations
must be assessed for the material actually distributed.

## Kokoro

The catalog's model identity is `kokoro-82m-v1`, version `1.0`; model assets are
`kokoro-v1.0.onnx` (SHA-256
`beb0d1848dee9a49da392cc3df26958d46cfa35d321edf434f52949153f0df3a`) and
`voices-v1.0.bin` (SHA-256
`bca610b8308e8d99f32e6fe4197e7ec01679264efed0cac9140fe9c29f1fbf7d`).
The [ONNX export release](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/tag/model-files-v1.1)
provides the assets; [hexgrad/Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M)
identifies the model as Apache-2.0. Preserve the Apache license and any applicable
notices and modified-file notices when redistributing. Confirm the export/style
asset provenance and exact associated model card rather than treating the MIT
runtime license as the weight license.

Model-card dataset attribution includes **Koniwa** (`tnc`, CC BY 3.0) and **SIWIS**
(CC BY 4.0): [Koniwa](https://github.com/koniwa/koniwa),
[SIWIS dataset](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353).
Retain these credits and the upstream model card with any redistributed assets.
No images from that model card are incorporated into this documentation.
