## Models, metadata and media

Piper voice metadata and model cards originate from
[rhasspy/piper-voices at the pinned revision](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/5041a7a49ae5b3a0f30e6837769eb03fdbb231bd).
The tracked snapshot preserves those cards and references; terms vary by model
and dataset. Some are noncommercial, research-only or incomplete. See the
[38-model audit](documentation/MODEL_LICENSE_AUDIT.md); no universal MIT/GPL grant
is asserted for them or for generated audio.

Kokoro-82M v1.0 is attributed to **hexgrad/Kokoro-82M**, with ONNX export by
**thewh1teagle**. The upstream model is Apache-2.0. Preserve the upstream model
card, applicable Apache notices and its dataset credits: **Koniwa** (`tnc`,
CC BY 3.0) and **SIWIS** (CC BY 4.0), with sources in the model audit.
Runtime licensing does not substitute for model/export/style-asset provenance.
No downloaded weights, external model-card images or voice audio are included here.

FFmpeg/FFprobe are optional external executables. The inspected Gyan 9.0.2
GPL/version3/static essentials build is not cleared by a generic upstream URL.
Before bundling, retain full applicable licenses and complete matching source,
configuration and patches. See [Licensing](documentation/LICENSING.md).
